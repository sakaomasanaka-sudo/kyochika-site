Nicowa Shin-Koiwa Festival 2026

Título en japonés: にこわ新小岩まつり2026

Nicowa Shin-Koiwa (Shin-Koiwa Community Center) (Est. Shin-Koiwa) · Gratis

Dance and music by local groups, crafts and paintings on show and for sale, and produce from Hanawa, Fukushima. A kids and parenting fair runs alongside. No registration needed.
