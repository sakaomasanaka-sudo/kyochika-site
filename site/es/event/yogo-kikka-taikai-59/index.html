59th Yogo Chrysanthemum Show

Título en japonés: 第59回影向菊花大会

Zenyoji Temple (Est. Koiwa)

About 1,000 pots of chrysanthemums, including large beds and cascades, fill Zenyoji Temple, famous for its Yogo pine. Plants on sale and growing advice throughout.
