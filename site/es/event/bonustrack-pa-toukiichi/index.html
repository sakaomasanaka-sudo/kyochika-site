P&A Pottery Market at BONUS TRACK

Título en japonés: P&A 陶器市 at BONUS TRACK

BONUS TRACK PARK (Est. Shimokitazawa) · Gratis

A pottery market with 18 makers and shops connected to the P&A pottery class. There is also a mini-object workshop.
