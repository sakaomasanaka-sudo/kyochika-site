21st Burari Ginza Autumn Sake Festival

Título en japonés: 第21回 ぶらり銀座 秋の酒まつり

Five prefectural shops around Ginza, incl. Hiroshima Brand Shop TAU (Est. Ginza-itchome)

Taste local sake at five prefectural shops around Ginza. October 16 is an evening pre-event.
