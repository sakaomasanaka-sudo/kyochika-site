59th Fukuro Matsuri / 27th Tokyo Yosakoi

Título en japonés: 第59回ふくろ祭り 第27回東京よさこい

Around Ikebukuro Station West Exit (main stage in front of the station and more) (Est. Ikebukuro)

More than 100 teams dance yosakoi in the streets of Ikebukuro. The official site gives no end time for the 10th.
