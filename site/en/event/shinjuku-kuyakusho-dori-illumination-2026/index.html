20th Shinjuku Kabukicho Kuyakusho-dori Illumination

Japanese title: 第20回 新宿歌舞伎町区役所通りイルミネーション

Kuyakusho-dori, Shinjuku (Yasukuni-dori to Shokuan-dori) (Shinjuku Sta.)

About 120,000 LEDs light up the trees along roughly 700 m of Kuyakusho-dori in this annual winter illumination. Lighting ceremony on Oct 30 from 16:30 in front of Shinjuku City Hall, with the Tokyo Fire Department Band.
