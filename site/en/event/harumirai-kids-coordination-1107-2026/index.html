Kids Coordination Training for Better Motor Skills (Harumirai)

Japanese title: 運動神経向上を目指す キッズコーディネーショントレーニング（はるみらい）

Harumirai (Harumi Community Center), Multipurpose Studio (Kachidoki Sta.)

Coordination training to help 4- to 6-year-olds control their bodies better. A parent or guardian must come along. Apply online from October 3 to November 6 (first 10 children).
