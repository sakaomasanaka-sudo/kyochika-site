Christmas Market at Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse 2026

Japanese title: Christmas Market in 横浜赤レンガ倉庫 2026

Yokohama Red Brick Warehouse Event Plaza & Red Brick Park (Nihon-odori Sta.)

A German-style Christmas market facing the sea and the Minato Mirai night view. Open until 21:00 from Nov 21 to Dec 4 and until 22:00 from Dec 5 (from 17:00 on Nov 20). Entry is 500 to 1,000 yen depending on the day, with priority tickets also sold; elementary school children and younger enter free (junior high and younger free until Dec 4). Dogs are allowed if fully inside a cage or cart. Held rain or shine.
