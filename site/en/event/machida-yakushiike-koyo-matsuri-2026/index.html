Yakushiike Autumn Leaves Festival (Machida)

Japanese title: 薬師池 紅葉まつり（町田薬師池公園四季彩の杜）

Machida Yakushiike Park Shikisai-no-Mori, Yakushi Pond (Machida Sta.)

Autumn leaves at Yakushi Pond. Nov 14–29 the park stays open until 20:00 with night illumination and a mist 'sea of clouds' show (hourly 10:00–19:00). Free guided tours on Nov 21–23, 28 and 29 (13:30 and 15:30, about 1 hour). Public transport is recommended as parking gets crowded.
