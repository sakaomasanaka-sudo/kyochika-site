New Year Dinner Buffet 2027 (Tokyo Bay Shiomi Prince Hotel)

Japanese title: ニューイヤーディナーブッフェ2027（東京ベイ潮見プリンスホテル）

Tokyo Bay Shiomi Prince Hotel, 1F TIDE TABLE Shiomi (Shiomi Sta.)

Four-night year-end and New Year dinner buffet (120 minutes, last entry 20:30). New Year dishes such as salt-crusted sea bream and boiled crab, plus a tuna-cutting show daily at 17:45 and 19:15. Toshikoshi soba on Dec 31; ozoni and an osechi corner Jan 1–3.
