Tennoz Canal Fes 2026 Autumn/Winter

Japanese title: 天王洲キャナルフェス2026秋冬

Tennozu Isle area (CANAL EAST, Tennoz Canal, Ail Shinagawa, Seafort Square) (Tennozu Isle Sta.) · Free

A market and food festival along the Tennozu canals. ART YATAI art-themed stalls, the handmade market HOME MADE, a STREET FOOD FESTIVAL and a charcoal grill fest line up, and on the 31st and 1st there are Kids' University workshops and police and fire vehicle displays (some need booking).
