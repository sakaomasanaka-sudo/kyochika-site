Ginchakai 2026: Ginza Outdoor Tea Gathering (Autumn Ginza)

Japanese title: 第24回 銀茶会2026（オータムギンザ）

Outdoor tea spots along Ginza-dori (Ginza 1–8) and Ginza Mitsukoshi 9F (Ginza Sta.) · Free

An autumn tea gathering where six tea ceremony schools and sencha masters serve tea at 12 outdoor spots along Ginza-dori, with original wagashi from Ginza shops. The beginner tea experience needs no booking. In rain, only the tea room on Ginza Mitsukoshi 9F is held.
