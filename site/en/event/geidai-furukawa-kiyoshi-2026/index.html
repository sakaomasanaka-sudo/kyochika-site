Kiyoshi Furukawa Retirement Exhibition: Resonating Sound and Media — Media Art Music Today

Japanese title: 古川聖退任記念展 共振する音とメディア〜メディアアート・ミュージックの現在〜

The University Art Museum, Tokyo University of the Arts — Chinretsukan Gallery 1F & 2F (Ueno Sta.) · Free

A retirement show for Professor Kiyoshi Furukawa of Intermedia Art, presenting his work from algorithmic composition and computer music to neuroscience, AI, and art and care through interactive works and documentary videos. Open daily during the exhibition.
