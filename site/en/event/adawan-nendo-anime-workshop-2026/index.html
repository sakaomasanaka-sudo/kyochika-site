Adawan Video Workshop: Let's Make a Clay Animation! (9th)

Japanese title: あだワン映像ワークショップ「第9回ねんどでアニメを作ろう！」

Tokyo Art Center, 2F White Studio (Kita-senju Sta.) · Free

A parent-and-child video workshop by Adachi City. Participants make a stop-motion animation with clay, which can be entered in the 60-second official CM contest "Adawan". Bring a smartphone or tablet. Apply online, by mail or by phone by Oct 28; a lottery is held if oversubscribed.
