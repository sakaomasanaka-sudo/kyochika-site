Rock Balancing Art “Ishihana” – Chitoku Ishihana Solo Exhibition

Japanese title: ロックバランシングアート「石花」―石が立つ、その瞬間。―（石花ちとく個展）

Gallery NOOOG (Okazu Yokocho, Torigoe) (Shin-Okachimachi Sta.) · Free

A solo show of rock balancing: natural stones stood on a single point of balance without glue or tools, seen as “stone flowers.”
