MES “レイ/RE-I”

Japanese title: MES「レイ/RE-I」

PARCO MUSEUM TOKYO (Shibuya PARCO 4F) (Shibuya Sta.) · Free

A solo show by artist duo MES. Starting from words read as “rei”, it brings up forgotten layers of Shibuya’s history through video installation and performance. Closes at 18:00 on the last day.
