Noragami POP UP SHOP (Kinokuniya Shinjuku Main Store)

Japanese title: 『ノラガミ』POP UP SHOP（紀伊國屋書店新宿本店）

Kinokuniya Shinjuku Main Store 1F, THE ENTRANCE (Shinjuku-sanchome Sta.)

A pop-up shop for the modern Japanese fantasy manga Noragami. One random ema-style card for every ¥3,300 (tax incl.) spent on eligible items. Closes at 16:00 on the last day, Dec 6.
