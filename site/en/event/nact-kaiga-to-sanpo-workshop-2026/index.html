NACT Artist Workshop "Painting and Walking!"

Japanese title: 国立新美術館 アーティスト・ワークショップ「絵画と散歩！大作戦」

The National Art Center, Tokyo (3F studio, etc.) (Roppongi Sta.) · Free

Families copy a painting chosen from Art Library books onto a board with artists Rintaro Hara and Yu Hara, then take it for a walk around the museum. 30 groups per session; wear clothes that can get dirty.
