Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay Winter Illumination "Precious Time Illumination"

Japanese title: ホテルオークラ東京ベイ ウィンターイルミネーション「Precious Time Illumination」

Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay (Maihama Sta.)

Winter illuminations at three spots on the grounds of Hotel Okura Tokyo Bay in Maihama: the Candle Gate, the Dome of Brilliance at the main entrance, and the courtyard. Lit from sunset until midnight.
