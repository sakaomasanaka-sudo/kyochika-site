Tobu Zoo Winter Illumination 2026-2027

Japanese title: 東武動物公園 ウインターイルミネーション2026-2027

Tobu Zoo (Tobu-Dobutsu-Koen Sta.)

A 65-day winter light show at the zoo-and-amusement park. Illumination tickets go on sale at 15:00 on the day. Closed Tue/Wed (except holidays) from 1/12–1/27 and in February. Unlimited-ride pass 2,500 yen.
