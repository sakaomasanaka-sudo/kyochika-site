Yum Cha Afternoon Tea 'Autumn Harvest & Hong Kong Delights' (The Westin Tokyo, Ryu Ten Mon)

Japanese title: 飲茶アフタヌーンティー「秋の収穫祭＆香港ディライツ」（ウェスティンホテル東京 龍天門）

The Westin Tokyo 2F, Cantonese restaurant Ryu Ten Mon (Ebisu Sta.)

Dim sum served from trolleys, traditional Hong Kong sweets and autumn desserts with chestnut and Shine Muscat grapes, enjoyed with Chinese tea. Weekdays two seatings (14:00 / 14:30), weekends and holidays one seating at 13:45, 2 hours each.
