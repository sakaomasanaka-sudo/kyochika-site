Fun Handmade Craft Corner, Autumn Season (Science Museum, Tokyo)

Japanese title: たのしい手作り工作体験コーナー 秋期（科学技術館）

Science Museum 3F lobby (by the stairs) (Takebashi Sta.)

Pick a base such as a clear candle, a mini cactus in seven colors of sand, a candy house that doubles as a trinket box, or clay fake sweets, and decorate it freely. Seasonal parts like Halloween are available. No booking; drop in until 16:00.
