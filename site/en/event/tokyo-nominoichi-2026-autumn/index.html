Tokyo Nominoichi '26 Autumn (Tokyo Flea Market)

Japanese title: 東京蚤の市’26 AUTUMN

Showa Kinen Park, Yume Hiroba (Tachikawa Sta.)

One of Tokyo's biggest flea markets, with 300+ vendors selling antiques, vintage clothing, used books, records and handmade crafts, plus food trucks and live stages. Sweet bean paste festival on 10/31–11/1 and wheat/bread festival on 11/2–11/3.
