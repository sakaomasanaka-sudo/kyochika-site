Tokyo Philharmonic Orchestra New Year Concert 2027

Japanese title: 東京フィルハーモニー交響楽団 ニューイヤーコンサート2027 ～どこかで出会った、あのメロディ～

Bunkamura Orchard Hall (Shibuya Sta.)

Starts 15:00 each day. Conducted by Kensho Watanabe, hosted by Satoshi Asaoka. Program includes the Die Fledermaus overture and Boléro, plus a "lucky bag" segment chosen by audience vote. The last New Year concert before Orchard Hall closes for renovation.
