Komazawa Wanko Matsuri 2026

Japanese title: 駒沢わんこ祭り2026

Komazawa Olympic Park Central Plaza (Komazawa-daigaku Sta.)

A dog festival with many free activities such as a speed-eating contest, pro photo sessions and paw spa (times vary by activity). Leash required; dogs must have rabies and 3+ combination vaccines. Held rain or shine.
