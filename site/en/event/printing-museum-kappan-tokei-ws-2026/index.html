Letterpress Workshop for Adults: "Printing Time - Letterpress Clock" (Printing Museum, Tokyo)

Japanese title: 大人のための活版ワークショップ「時を刷る 活版時計」（印刷博物館）

Printing Museum, Tokyo - Printing Studio (Edogawabashi Sta.)

Set a forme with Latin numeral type, letterpress-print a clock face and turn it into a clock. Same content on Saturday and Sunday; join one session. Apply via web form only, Oct 27 10:00 - Nov 4 18:00; lottery.
