Lovit! Gourmet Festival

Japanese title: ラヴィット！グルメフェスティバル

Ginza Mitsukoshi, Annex 7F Event Hall (Ginza Sta.)

Food fair from the TBS morning show 'Lovit!', held at Ginza Mitsukoshi for the first time. Part 1: Oct 28-Nov 2; Part 2: Nov 3-9. Napolitan pasta, gyoza, curry, sweets and more, with eat-in (last order 60 min before closing). Closes 17:00 on Nov 2 and Nov 9; entry limits or numbered tickets when crowded.
