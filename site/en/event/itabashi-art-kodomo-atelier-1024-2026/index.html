Kids' Atelier: "Traveling Magic Pictures" (Itabashi Art Museum)

Japanese title: こどもアトリエ「旅する魔法の絵」（板橋区立美術館）

Itabashi Art Museum (Nishi-takashimadaira Sta.)

With artist さわせたり, cut and paste colored paper to create travel scenery, finished with a pull-tab that makes the scene appear. Two sessions: 10:00-12:00 and 14:00-16:00. Book by phone at 03-3979-3251.
