Koishikawa Korakuen Special Night Opening

Japanese title: 夜間特別開園「小石川後楽園 深山光葉を楽しむ」

Koishikawa Korakuen Gardens (enter via West Gate) (Iidabashi Sta.)

Light-up of the garden (incl. snow-suspension ropes) and projection mapping on the Engetsu-kyo bridge and earthen wall. Buying the night ticket in advance is recommended (last entry 20:00). Tea house and kitchen cars on site.
