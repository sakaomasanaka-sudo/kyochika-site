Edogawa Sportsland Ice Skating Rink (2026–27 Season)

Japanese title: 江戸川区スポーツランド アイススケートリンク（2026-2027シーズン）

Edogawa Sportsland Ice Skating Rink (Shinozaki Sta.)

The only ward-run ice rink in Tokyo's 23 wards, open Oct 1, 2026 to May 31, 2027. Public skating Mon/Wed/Fri 9:00–18:00 and weekends/holidays 10:00–18:00 (no public skating Tue/Thu). Check the official site for year-end hours.
