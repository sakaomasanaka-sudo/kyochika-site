Shogo Kariyazaki: Small Arrangements in His Vase Collection (40th Anniversary, 3rd edition)

Japanese title: 華道歴40周年記念 第3回「假屋崎省吾 花器コレクションに小品をいける」

Shogo Kariyazaki Flower School, Ginza (Dai-7 Central Bldg. 4F-A) (Shimbashi Sta.)

A solo show marking flower artist Shogo Kariyazaki's 40 years in ikebana, with small arrangements displayed in vases from his own collection, held at his flower school in Ginza.
