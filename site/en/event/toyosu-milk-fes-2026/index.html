Milk Smile Project: MILK FES in Toyosu 2026

Japanese title: 牛乳でスマイルプロジェクト ミルクフェス in 豊洲 2026

Toyosu Park, Flower Tree & Monument Plaza (Toyosu Sta.) · Free

Taste two milks from the 47 prefectures of Japan, try yogurt, see real cows, hear dairy farmers talk, and join quizzes and games on the mini stage. Held in light rain; cancelled in storms.
