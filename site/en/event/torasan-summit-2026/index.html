Tora-san Summit 2026

Japanese title: 寅さんサミット2026

Shibamata Taishakuten approach (main venue), Tora-san Museum, Yamamoto-tei and more (Shibamata Sta.) · Free

22 filming locations of the Otoko wa Tsurai yo (Tora-san) films gather in Shibamata to show off local performing arts, specialties and food. Held rain or shine.
