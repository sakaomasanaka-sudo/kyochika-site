Tokyo Canicross 2026 (Dog Marathon)

Japanese title: 2026東京カニクロス（ドッグマラソン）

Mizumoto Park Central Plaza (Kanamachi Sta.) · Free

A dog marathon where you run 1.5 km or 3 km on grass with your dog. Free to watch, with a market and food trucks from 10:00. A vet is on site. Held rain or shine.
