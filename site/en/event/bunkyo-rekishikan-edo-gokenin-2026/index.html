Special Exhibition: Gokenin of Edo – The Samurai Who Supported the Shogunate

Japanese title: 区制80周年記念特別展「江戸の御家人 幕府を支えたサムライたち」

Bunkyo Museum (Hongo-sanchome Sta.)

A special exhibition tracing the work and lives of the gokenin retainers who ran the shogunate's daily business, through manuals and drawings they kept. Gallery talks need no booking.
