Imawa Itsu no Koto ka: Komura Settai and Takuro Tamayama

Japanese title: 今はいつのことか―小村雪岱と玉山拓郎

Shiseido Gallery (Tokyo Ginza Shiseido Bldg. B1) (Shimbashi Sta.) · Free

An exhibition featuring Taisho–Showa era book designer and illustrator Komura Settai alongside contemporary artist Takuro Tamayama. Free. Open until 19:00 (18:00 on Sundays and holidays). Closed Mondays (including holidays) and Dec 21–Jan 11.
