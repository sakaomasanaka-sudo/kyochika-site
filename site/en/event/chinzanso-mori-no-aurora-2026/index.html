Forest Aurora and Sparkling Drops (Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo)

Japanese title: 森のオーロラと雫の煌めき（ホテル椿山荘東京）

Hotel Chinzanso Tokyo garden (around Yusui Pond) (Edogawabashi Sta.)

See the "Forest Aurora" and the "Tokyo Unkai" sea-of-clouds mist together in the winter garden. Shows at 18:40, 20:40 and 21:40 (about 7 min each); tree night shows at 19:40 and 21:10.
