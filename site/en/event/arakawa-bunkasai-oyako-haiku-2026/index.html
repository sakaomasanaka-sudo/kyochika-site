Arakawa City Culture Festival: Parent-and-Child Haiku Class

Japanese title: 荒川区文化祭「親子俳句教室」

Machiya Culture Center, 3F Meeting Room 4 (Machiya Sta.) · Free

The Arakawa Haiku Federation teaches how to write haiku to parents and children. For grades 1–6 living or studying in Arakawa City with a guardian, 20 pairs. Apply at the counter, by phone or email by Oct 28, 17:00; bring writing tools.
