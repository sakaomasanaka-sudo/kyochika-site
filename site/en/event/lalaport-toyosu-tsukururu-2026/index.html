Tsukururu: Make Art, Make a Town (LaLaport Toyosu)

Japanese title: アートをつくる、まちをつくる つくるる（アーバンドック ららぽーと豊洲）

Urban Dock LaLaport Toyosu 1F North Port / South Port Event Space (Toyosu Sta.) · Free

A free art event for children where kids create works with professional artists and creators. Drop-in coloring and crafts use same-day numbered tickets; badge, picture-book and clay workshops and portrait sessions need advance booking. Finished works are displayed together as a shared town.
