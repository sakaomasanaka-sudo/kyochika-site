Food Collection: A Gourmet Stroll Through Lively Shopping Streets

Japanese title: 【フードコレクション】人情と活気に溢れる商店街、ぶらりグルメ旅

Nihombashi Mitsukoshi Main Store, B1 Food Collection (Mitsukoshimae Sta.)

Shops from shopping streets across Japan such as Togoshi Ginza, Yanaka Ginza, Azabu-Juban and Kawagoe gather at the B1 food hall (onigiri, gyoza, karaage, bagels, okonomiyaki and more). Eat-in only for the Azabu-Juban mont blanc; last order 19:00 (18:30 on the last day).
