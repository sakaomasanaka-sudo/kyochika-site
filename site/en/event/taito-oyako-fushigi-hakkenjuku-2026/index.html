Parent-Child Wonder Discovery Workshop (Taito City)

Japanese title: 親子ふしぎ発見塾～楽しく遊ぼう～（台東区）

Taito City Office 10F Meeting Room (Ueno Sta.) · Free

Parents and kids make a "magical ring," a "catch-me bug" and a "mystery texture ball" with guidance from youth commissioners. For grades 3–6 with one guardian (pairs), 100 pairs. Apply online by Nov 5; lottery if oversubscribed.
