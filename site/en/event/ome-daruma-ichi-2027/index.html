Ome Daruma Market 2027

Japanese title: 青梅だるま市 2027

Old Ome Kaido road by Ome Station (Ome Sta.)

A daruma market held every January 12 on the old Ome Kaido road near Ome Station. Market 13:30–20:00, with traffic restrictions 13:00–21:00. The date is per the Tokyo official tourism site ("every January 12").
