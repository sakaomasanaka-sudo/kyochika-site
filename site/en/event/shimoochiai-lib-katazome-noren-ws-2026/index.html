Make a Noren Curtain for Some no Komichi: Stencil-Dyeing Workshop (Shimo-Ochiai Library)

Japanese title: 染の小道に飾る暖簾を作ろう。型染体験！（下落合図書館）

Shinjuku City Shimo-Ochiai Library Multipurpose Room (Shimo-Ochiai Sta.)

Stencil-dye a cloth with an instructor from Ochiai's dyeing district; the pieces are later displayed at the Some no Komichi festival. 10 places, first come first served from Oct 28 at the library counter or by phone.
