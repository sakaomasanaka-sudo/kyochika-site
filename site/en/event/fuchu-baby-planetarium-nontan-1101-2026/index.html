Special Baby Planetarium: "Genki Genki Nontan Kirakira Shanshan Ohoshisama" (Fuchu City Museum of Local History)

Japanese title: 特別投映ベビープラネタリウム「げんき げんき ノンタン きらきら シャンシャン おほしさま☆」（府中市郷土の森博物館）

Fuchu City Museum of Local History (Kyodo-no-Mori) Planetarium (Bubaigawara Sta.)

A special show for small children and families: doors 10:05, starts 10:15, 30 minutes. The dome is not made fully dark, so there is no star commentary. On Nov 22 (Sun), "with the star sister and brother" plays at the same time.
