Stuffed Animal Storytime (Umeda Library)

Japanese title: ぬいぐるみおはなし会（梅田図書館）

Umeda Library 2F, Carpet Corner (Umejima Sta.) · Free

A storytime that children join together with their favorite stuffed animal; bring your own. Up to 10 groups; apply from Oct 10 at the library counter or by phone (03-3840-4646). Reception opens at 14:45.
