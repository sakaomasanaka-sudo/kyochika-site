Let's enjoy ORIGAMI (Asakusa Culture Tourist Information Center)

Japanese title: Let's enjoy ORIGAMI（浅草文化観光センター）

Asakusa Culture Tourist Information Center 7F (Asakusa Sta.) · Free

A monthly origami session on the third Tuesday at the tourist center opposite Kaminarimon. About 15 minutes each, no reservation needed. Easy for overseas visitors too.
