Gelato Collection 2026 (Craft Gelato Festival)

Japanese title: Gelato Collection 2026（クラフトジェラートの祭典）

FMI Tokyo Head Office, 1F Test Kitchen (Kamiyacho Sta.)

The second edition of a craft gelato festival bringing together up-and-coming gelato makers from across Japan. Taste about 20 gelatos freely and vote for the gold prize. Four sessions (10:00-11:30 / 11:30-13:00 / 14:00-15:30 / 15:30-17:00); advance tickets required via Peatix. 4 min walk from Kamiyacho Station.
