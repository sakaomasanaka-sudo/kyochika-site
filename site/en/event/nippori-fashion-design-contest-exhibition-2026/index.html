Nippori Fashion Design Contest 2026 Public Exhibition

Japanese title: 日暮里ファッションデザインコンテスト2026 公開展示会

Furatto Nippori (Nippori Community Revitalization Facility) (Nippori Sta.)

Shows 40 award-winning and selected garments chosen from 4,310 entries to the Nippori Textile Town fashion design contest. Last entry 17:45. During the show, the first 700 people presenting a receipt of ¥2,000+ from participating textile shops get a gift.
