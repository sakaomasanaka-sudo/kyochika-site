MIDTOWN YAESU CHRISTMAS 2026

Japanese title: MIDTOWN YAESU CHRISTMAS 2026

Tokyo Midtown Yaesu 1F Galleria, Main Entrance and more (Tokyo Sta.) · Free

A Christmas display directly connected to Tokyo Station that expresses Yaesu's history and canals in light. "MIRAGE VOYAGE" in the 1F Galleria runs a light-and-sound show every 10 minutes. "YAESU WINTER LIGHTS" stays lit until Feb 14, 2027.
