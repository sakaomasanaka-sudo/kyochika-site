34th Jimbocho Book Festival

Japanese title: 第34回 神保町ブックフェスティバル

Kanda Suzuran-dori, Sakura-dori & Jimbocho Mitsui Building plaza (Jimbocho Sta.)

Publishers set up stalls along Suzuran-dori and Sakura-dori to sell books. Food wagons and a stage add to the fun; the Children's Book Plaza and food court close at 17:00. Cancelled if it rains.
