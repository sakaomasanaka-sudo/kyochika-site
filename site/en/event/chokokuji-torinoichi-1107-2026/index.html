Chokokuji Temple Tori-no-Ichi (First Rooster Day), Asakusa

Japanese title: 浅草酉の寺 長國寺 酉の市（一の酉）

Chokokuji Temple (Senzoku, Asakusa) (Iriya Sta.)

The Tori-no-Ichi fair at Chokokuji, the 'Rooster Temple' next to Otori Shrine, open a full day from midnight to midnight with lucky rakes, amulets and food stalls. This year has two Rooster Days.
