Read-aloud Lecture 'Opening the World of Picture Encyclopedias Together' (Ota City Library)

Japanese title: 読み聞かせ講演会「親子でひらく 図鑑のせかい」（大田区立図書館）

Denenchofu Seseragi-kan Multipurpose Room (Tamagawa Sta.) · Free

Botanist Kenichi Saiki of the Natural History Museum and Institute, Chiba, who has collected over 2,000 illustrated encyclopedias, shares their appeal with parents and children. 50 seats; apply via online form or return postcard (postcards must arrive by Nov 15; lottery if oversubscribed, with priority to those living, studying or working in Ota City).
