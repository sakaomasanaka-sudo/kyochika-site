Wan-Fes.with 2026: A Festival to Enjoy with Your Dog

Japanese title: Wan-Fes.with 2026 〜わんこと楽しむフェスティバル〜

National Olympics Memorial Youth Center (Sangubashi Sta.)

A dog festival where your dog can sit next to you in an indoor hall normally closed to dogs to watch the stage. Also a dog marathon, runway, disaster-preparedness seminar and manners class.
