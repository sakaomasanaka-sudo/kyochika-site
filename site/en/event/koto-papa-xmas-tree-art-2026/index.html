Dads' Parenting Class: Christmas Tree Art with Your Child

Japanese title: パパのハッピー子育て講座～親子で楽しむクリスマスツリーアート～

Koto Gender Equality Promotion Center (Parcity Koto) 1F Rec Hall (Sumiyoshi Sta.) · Free

Using clinical art techniques, dads and kids make Christmas tree artwork while enjoying textures and colors. 16 pairs, first come; apply from Nov 5, 9:00. Free childcare for siblings (book by Dec 3).
