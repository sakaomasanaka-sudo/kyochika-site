Nippori Blue-Sky Children's Festival

Japanese title: にっぽり青空こどもまつり

Nippori Minami Park (Nippori Sta.)

One of Arakawa's children's festivals: dance and taiko performances, game and craft corners, new-sports try-outs and food stalls. Cancelled if it rains.
