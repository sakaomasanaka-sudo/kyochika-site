Katsushika Great Iris Exhibition (Part 2)

Japanese title: 特別展「葛飾区大花菖蒲展」第2期

Katsushika City Museum, 2F Special Exhibition Room (Ohanajaya Sta.)

The history of Katsushika's irises, from Edo-period cultivation and Horikiri Iris Garden to exports abroad, told through ukiyo-e, postcards and old photos. Open until 21:00 on Fri/Sat (non-holidays).
