Alter Ego (Akaji Maro × Éric-Maria Couturier)

Japanese title: Alter Ego（麿赤兒×エリック=マリア・クテュリエ）

Theatre Tram (Sangenjaya Sta.)

A Japan-France work in which Akaji Maro and Dairakudakan butoh dancers read Bach's cello suites, played live, through the body. Post-show talk on the 30th.
