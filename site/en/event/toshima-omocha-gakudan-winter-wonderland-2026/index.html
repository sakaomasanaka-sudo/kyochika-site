Toy Band Winter Wonderland: Play Christmas with Hinemos!

Japanese title: おもちゃの楽団のウィンターワンダーランド ーヒネモスと、クリスマスを遊ぼう！ー

Toshima Civic Center 8F Multipurpose Hall (Ikebukuro Sta.)

A Christmas concert by Hinemos, who play toys, melodicas and junk. Crying and moving around are OK. Kids who join the 10:30 parent-child workshop make a cymbal doll and take part in the afternoon show.

Dates
Dec 19 (Sat) – Dec 20 (Sun)
Time
14:00 start (expected to end around 15:30)
Price
