Toyosu Festa × Toyosu Library: Storytime and Halloween Crafts

Japanese title: 豊洲フェスタ×豊洲図書館「おはなし会とハロウィン工作を楽しもう！」

Toyosu Library 11F Story Room Kids (Toyosu Civic Center) (Toyosu Sta.)

After a volunteer storytime, kids make Halloween crafts: a ghost poncho and a witch's broom. Sessions at 13:00 and 14:00, 50 minutes each, about 30 people per session. No registration; just come to the venue.
