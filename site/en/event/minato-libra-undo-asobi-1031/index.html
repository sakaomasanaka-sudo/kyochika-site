Play Your Way! Movement Play for Kids (Libra, Minato)

Japanese title: 自分らしく楽しむ！運動遊び 第2回みんなであそぼう！（リーブラ）

Minato City Gender Equality Center Libra Hall (Minato Park Shibaura 1F) (Tamachi Sta.) · Free

A session where preschoolers and parents move and play together. Apply by phone, fax, web or in person; 20 pairs (siblings count as one). Childcare by reservation until Oct 15.
