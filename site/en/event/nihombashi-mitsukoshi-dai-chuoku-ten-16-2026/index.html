16th 'Heart of Tokyo' Chuo City Food & Craft Fair

Japanese title: 第16回 東京まん真ん中 味と匠の大中央区展

Nihombashi Mitsukoshi Main Store, Main Bldg 7F Event Hall (Mitsukoshimae Sta.)

A fair at Nihombashi Mitsukoshi gathering long-established and new food brands and crafts from Chuo City. Mostly food, plus kimono and lifestyle goods. Eat-in last order 30 min before closing; closes 18:00 on the last day.
