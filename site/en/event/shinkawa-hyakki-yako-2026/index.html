Shinkawa Hyakki Yako (Night Parade of Yokai)

Japanese title: 新川さくら館 新川百鬼夜行

Shinkawa Sakura-kan (Funabori Sta.) · Free

Yokai parade along the Shinkawa canal from 15:00, yokai boat rides, a yokai fashion show and talk, and a ghost-story session.
