Cutlassfish Day Talk with "Sakana no Onii-san" Kawachan & Sea Lion Performance (Sunshine Aquarium)

Japanese title: 11月11日はタチウオの日 制定記念 さかなのおにいさんかわちゃんのタチウオトーク＆アシカパフォーマンス（サンシャイン水族館）

Sunshine Aquarium, Marine Garden performance stage (outdoor area) (Higashi-ikebukuro Sta.)

On Nov 7 (Sat) and Nov 11 (Wed), 10:30–11:15, fish presenter Kawachan and keepers talk about cutlassfish, with a cutlassfish quiz for kids, ending with a dance together with Kawachan and the sea lions. The commemorative event runs Oct 9–Nov 15 with coloring displays and a cutlassfish exhibit.
