ORIX Presents New Year Concert 2027 (New Japan Philharmonic)

Japanese title: ORIX Presents ニューイヤー・コンサート2027（新日本フィル）

Sumida Triphony Hall, Main Hall (Kinshicho Sta.)

A New Year concert conducted by Sachio Fujioka: Strauss waltzes, opera arias and a Vivaldi concerto, plus a shiko stomp demonstration by sumo wrestlers of the Naruto stable. Elementary school age and up.
