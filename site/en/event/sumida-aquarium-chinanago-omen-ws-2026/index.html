Garden Eel Mask Making (Sumida Aquarium)

Japanese title: チンアナゴお面づくり（すみだ水族館「※チンアナゴではありま展」）

Sumida Aquarium 5F Aqua Academy (Oshiage (Skytree) Sta.)

Pick one of three eel masks and color it in. Weekdays 11:00–16:00 (last entry 15:30), weekends and holidays 10:00–17:00 (last entry 16:30). Some days, such as Nov 13, close early for private bookings.
