THAT'S FASHION WEEKEND 2026 Charity Fashion Market

Japanese title: THAT'S FASHION WEEKEND 2026 チャリティファッションマーケット

Tokyo Tower, Tower Hall A (Shibakoen Sta.) · Free

A charity market selling about 7,000 items from popular brands at 70–90% off, including JIL SANDER, RICK OWENS and DRIES VAN NOTEN. For every item bought, one new garment is donated to disaster-hit areas of Kumamoto. A charity flea market runs at the same time. Closes at 15:30 on the last day.
