3rd Chuo City Harumi Festival

Japanese title: 第3回 中央区晴海まつり

Harumi Community Center 'Harumirai' & Harumi Pier Park (Kachidoki Sta.) · Free

Fair-game corner (reservation needed), candle crafts with children's center staff, kids' mikoshi carrying, projection-mapping movement games, inflatables and a mini train. Held rain or shine (may be cancelled depending on conditions).
