Katsushika Philharmonic Members Concert at a Sento (Tomi-no-yu)

Japanese title: 銭湯で葛飾フィルハーモニー管弦楽団メンバーの演奏会（富の湯）

Tomi-no-yu public bath (Tateishi) (Keisei-Tateishi Sta.) · Free

Members of the Katsushika Philharmonic play classical pieces, film music, children's songs and Japanese pop in Tomi-no-yu, a public bathhouse in Tateishi. Doors 12:30.
