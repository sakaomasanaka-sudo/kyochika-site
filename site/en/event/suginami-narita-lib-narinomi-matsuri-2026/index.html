Narinomi Festival (Narita Library)

Japanese title: なりのみまつり（成田図書館）

Suginami City Narita Library (Minami-Asagaya Sta.)

A children's festival at Narita Library with panel theater, big picture books, warabe-uta songs, finger plays, storytimes, quizzes, drawing songs, a puppet show and film screenings, held in the 2F meeting room and the 1F story room. Some programs give priority to advance applicants; walk-ins join if there is space.
