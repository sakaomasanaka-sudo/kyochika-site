Autumn Color Lantern Making (Koganei Park "Shuusai Monogatari" Workshop)

Japanese title: 秋彩ランタン作り（小金井公園「秋彩ものがたり」ワークショップ）

Koganei Park (around Ikoi-no-Hiroba and Edo-Tokyo Open Air Architectural Museum) (Musashi-Koganei Sta.)

A workshop of the flower-and-light event "Shuusai Monogatari". Arrange fallen leaves gathered in the park, laminate them and attach to a wooden frame to make a lantern. Held Oct 23, Oct 31 and Nov 7. Book via the reservation form. Preschoolers must come with a guardian.
