Setagaya Boro-ichi Market (January)

Japanese title: 世田谷のボロ市（1月）

Boro-ichi Street area, Setagaya 1-chome (Kamimachi Sta.) · Free

A market with over 440 years of history, held every Dec and Jan on the 15th and 16th. Stalls selling used clothing, antiques, plants and food line Boro-ichi Street. No parking; may be cancelled in bad weather.
