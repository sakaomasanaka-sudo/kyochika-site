Chiikawa Christmas Market

Japanese title: ちいかわクリスマスマーケット

Toyosuna Park (in front of AEON Mall Makuhari Shintoshin) (Makuhari-Toyosuna Sta.)

A roughly 6,000 m² Christmas market with Chiikawa and friends, featuring limited-edition food and goods. Date-and-time tickets are required; the last entry slot is 18:30-19:00. Children under 4 do not need a ticket (one per adult).
