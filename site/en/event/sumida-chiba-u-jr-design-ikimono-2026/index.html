Sumida x Chiba University Junior Design School for Teens: "3D Models of Living Creatures"

Japanese title: 墨田区×千葉大学 中高生向けジュニアデザインスクール『生きものの立体モデルづくり』

Chiba University Sumida Satellite Campus 1F, Model Shop (Higashi-azuma Sta.)

A single-session course where, with support from Chiba University students, participants create 3D models of living creatures on a 3D printer from data they make themselves. For junior and senior high school students (including technical college years 1-3); works handed over later.
