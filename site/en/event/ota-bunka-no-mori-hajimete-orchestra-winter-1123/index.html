Orchestra for Ages 0 and Up: Winter Concert (Ota Bunka no Mori)

Japanese title: 0歳からの・はじめてのオーケストラ 冬のコンサート（大田文化の森）

Ota Bunka no Mori 5F, Multipurpose Room (Omori Sta.)

An interactive family concert by Salon Orchestra Japan. Doors open at 14:00 with toy-instrument play and a violin try-out; the concert starts at 14:30 with a "Let's Waltz!" corner (The Skaters' Waltz and more). Nursing room available. Early-bird prices until Nov 7. About 16 minutes' walk from Omori Station West Exit.
