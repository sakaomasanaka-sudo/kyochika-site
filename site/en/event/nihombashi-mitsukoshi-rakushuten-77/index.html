77th Kyoto Specialties Fair 'Rakushuten'

Japanese title: 第77回 京名物 洛趣展

Nihombashi Mitsukoshi Main Store, Main Bldg 7F event hall (2nd venue: 4F kimono) (Mitsukoshimae Sta.)

The 77th fair of long-established Kyoto shops, marking 400 years since the Kan'ei imperial visit: Japanese sweets, Kyoto foods, crafts and kimono accessories, with Kyoto cuisine in the eat-in corner and live demonstrations.
