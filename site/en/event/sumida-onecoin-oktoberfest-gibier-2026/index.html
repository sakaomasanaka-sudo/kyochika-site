Sumida One-Coin Oktoberfest / Gibier Festival

Japanese title: すみだワンコインオクトーバーフェスト／ジビエフェスティバル

Kinshi Park Fureai Plaza (Kinshicho Sta.)

Sample craft beers from several breweries for one coin each. Also game dishes such as venison, wild boar and whale, other food and drinks, a market and live music.
