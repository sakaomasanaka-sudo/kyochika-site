Yebisu Garden Place Illumination & Christmas Marché 2026

Japanese title: 恵比寿ガーデンプレイス イルミネーション＆クリスマスマルシェ 2026

Yebisu Garden Place Clock Plaza & Château Plaza (Ebisu Sta.)

A marché inspired by European Christmas markets (Clock Plaza from Nov 14, Château Plaza from Nov 27; weekdays 17:00–20:00, weekends and holidays 12:00–20:00). A 10 m tree stands in Clock Plaza, and the illumination runs 16:00–23:00 until Feb 28, 2027. May be cancelled in rain or strong wind.
