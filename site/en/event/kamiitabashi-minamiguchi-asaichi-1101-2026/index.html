Kami-itabashi South Exit Morning Market

Japanese title: 上板橋南口朝市マルシェ

Kami-itabashi Minamiguchi Ginza Shopping Street (Kami-itabashi Sta.)

A morning market held on the first Sunday of each month in the shopping street by Kami-itabashi Station's south exit, with bargains on fresh food and household goods.
