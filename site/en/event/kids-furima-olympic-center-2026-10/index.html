Kids Flea Market at National Olympics Memorial Youth Center

Japanese title: キッズフリマ in 国立オリンピック記念青少年総合センター

National Olympics Memorial Youth Center, Reception Hall (Sangubashi Sta.) · Free

A flea market run only by children, buying and selling with real money in an area adults can't enter, to learn the value of things and money. Two sessions daily: 11:00-12:00 and 14:30-15:30. Free for buyers (booking slots from Oct 20, or walk in). Indoor.
