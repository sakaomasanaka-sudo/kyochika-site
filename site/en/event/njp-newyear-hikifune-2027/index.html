New Japan Philharmonic New Year Concert 2027 in Sumida Hikifune: Rakugo & Orchestra

Japanese title: 新日本フィル ニューイヤー・コンサート2027 in すみだ曳舟《落語＆オーケストラ》

Hikifune Culture Center, Theatre Hall (Keisei-Hikifune Sta.)

Part 1 is rakugo by Kikuhiko Hayashiya; Part 2 is the New Japan Philharmonic under Sachio Fujioka (soprano Ayano Nonomura) with the Die Fledermaus overture, Ifukube's SF Symphonic Fantasy No. 1 and more. Doors 14:30, starts 15:00. Preschool children not admitted.
