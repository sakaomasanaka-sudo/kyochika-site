Autumn Flower Market

Japanese title: Autumn Flower Market（オタフラ）

Rental Space Sakura Nakameguro (Meguro River) (Naka-meguro Sta.) · Free

A flower-themed market of handmade artists by the Meguro River. Free entry; walk-in from 15:30 on the 24th and from 14:00 on the 25th (earlier slots need booking).
