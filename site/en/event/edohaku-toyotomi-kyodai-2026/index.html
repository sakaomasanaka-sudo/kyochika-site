Special Exhibition "The Toyotomi Brothers!" (Edo-Tokyo Museum)

Japanese title: 江戸東京博物館リニューアル記念・NHK大河ドラマ特別展「豊臣兄弟！」

Edo-Tokyo Museum, 1F Special Exhibition Gallery (Ryogoku Sta.)

A special exhibition at the renovated Edo-Tokyo Museum tracing Toyotomi Hideyoshi and his brother Hidenaga, tied to the NHK Taiga drama. Open until 19:30 on Saturdays; last entry 30 min before closing.
