What a Wonderful World: Masterworks from the Musée d'Orsay

Japanese title: 東京都美術館開館100周年記念 オルセー美術館所蔵 いまを生きる歓び

Tokyo Metropolitan Art Museum, Special Exhibition Gallery (Ueno Sta.)

About 110 works from the Musée d'Orsay, including Millet, Renoir, Monet and Van Gogh, showing everyday life and joy in the 19th century. Open until 20:00 on Fridays.
