Craft Beer Japan Hop Fest 2026 at SVB Tokyo

Japanese title: クラフトビール ジャパンホップ フェスト2026 in SVB東京

Spring Valley Brewery Tokyo (Daikanyama Sta.)

Craft beers made with up to 20 varieties of Japanese hops, including fresh-hop beers from this year's harvest, with a sushi set for advance-ticket holders. Three sessions a day: 11:00–13:30, 14:30–17:00 and 18:00–20:30. Drinking age is 20.
