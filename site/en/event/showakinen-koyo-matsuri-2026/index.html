Autumn Leaves Festival & Autumn Night Walk 2026 (Showa Kinen Park)

Japanese title: 黄葉・紅葉まつり＆秋の夜散歩2026（国営昭和記念公園）

Showa Kinen Park (Nishi-Tachikawa Sta.)

The ginkgo avenues along the Canal and Katarai paths and the maples of the Japanese Garden are lit up from 16:30 to 20:30. The Japanese Garden at night needs a separate ticket (Oct 29–31 park admission only). At night, entry is via the Nishi-Tachikawa and Tachikawa gates only.

Dates
Oct 29 (Thu) – Nov 29 (Sun)

09:30–21:00
Price
