Setagaya Art Town 2026: Sancha de Daidogei

Japanese title: 世田谷アートタウン2026 三茶de大道芸

Sangenjaya Fureai Hiroba, Chazawa-dori and around Sangenjaya Station (Sangenjaya Sta.)

An autumn street-performance festival that turns all of Sangenjaya into a stage. Shows and food stalls at Fureai Hiroba both days; on the 18th, Chazawa-dori is closed to cars for performers.
