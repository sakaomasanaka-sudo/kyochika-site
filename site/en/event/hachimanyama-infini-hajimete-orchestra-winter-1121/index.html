Orchestra for Ages 0 and Up: Winter Concert (Infini Theater, Hachimanyama)

Japanese title: 0歳からの・はじめてのオーケストラ 冬のコンサート（アンフィニシアター・杉並八幡山）

Infini Theater (Oto Oasis 1F) (Hachimanyama Sta.)

An interactive concert for families from age 0. Two shows: doors 13:50 / start 14:10 and doors 15:10 / start 15:30, with toy-instrument play and a violin try-out before each. Mat seats at the front and chairs at the back. Nursing, diaper-changing and stroller space available. Early-bird prices until Nov 1.
