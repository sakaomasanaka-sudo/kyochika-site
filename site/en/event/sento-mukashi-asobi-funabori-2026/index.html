Sento & Old-Time Games

Japanese title: 銭湯と！昔あそびの会

Tower Hall Funabori 3F, Industrial Promotion Center (Funabori Sta.) · Free

A public bathhouse exhibit plus traditional play like chopstick pop guns and crafts. Every child gets a gift; play two or more games to spin a gacha. Sessions at 11:00 and 12:30.
