WHAT CAFE x Miyamoto Unosuke Shoten "Matsuri x Art Exhibition"

Japanese title: WHAT CAFE×宮本卯之助商店「Matsuri × Art Exhibition -祭の多面体-」

WHAT CAFE (Tennoz) (Tennozu Isle Sta.) · Free

A collaboration between WHAT CAFE and Miyamoto Unosuke Shoten, a long-established maker of festival instruments. Thirteen artists, including Keigo Kamide, Noritaka Tatehana and Hiromine Nakamura, reinterpret Japanese festivals through contemporary art. Open until 21:00 on Oct 30-31 during Tennoz Canal Fes. Free.
