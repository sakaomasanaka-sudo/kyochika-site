Tokyo Fire Department New Year Parade 2027 (Ariake)

Japanese title: 令和9年 東京消防出初式（有明）

Ariake Garden (exhibition) / Tokyo Rinkai Disaster Prevention Park (ceremony) (Ariake Sta.) · Free

The Tokyo Fire Department's annual New Year review. The ceremony at Tokyo Rinkai Disaster Prevention Park (about 10:00–12:00) requires advance application; the exhibition at Ariake Garden is open 10:00–15:00, free.
