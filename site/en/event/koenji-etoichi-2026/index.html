21st Autumn Etoile Festival "Etoichi"

Japanese title: 第21回 秋のエトアール祭「エトいち」

Etoile-dori Shopping Street (around Chosenji Temple) (Koenji Sta.)

A shopping-street autumn festival in south Koenji: a market of handmade goods and flea stalls on the Chosenji temple approach, street performances (24th), Awa Odori dance and rakugo (25th).
