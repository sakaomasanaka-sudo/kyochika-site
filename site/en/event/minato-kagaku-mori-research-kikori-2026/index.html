Research the Forest! Try Nature Surveys and Woodcutting (Minato Science Museum)

Japanese title: 森をリサーチ！自然環境調査と木こりを体験しよう（みなと科学館）

Minato City Science Museum, Lab (Toranomon Sta.) · Free

Quizzes on wildlife in Minato's forest in Akiruno, a sensor-camera activity, and sawing thinned logs like a woodcutter (90 min at 10:30 and 14:00, 9 pairs each). Lottery closed Oct 13; open seats go first-come until 23:59 the day before.
