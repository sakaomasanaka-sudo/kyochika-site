Museum with Your Baby (Ota City Folk Museum)

Japanese title: 赤ちゃんと一緒に博物館（大田区立郷土博物館）

Ota City Folk Museum (meeting room & special display corner) (Nishi-magome Sta.) · Free

A curator's gallery talk on the special display about writer Uno Chiyo, plus parent-child play and picture-book reading for babies. Eight pairs, first-come; phone booking from Oct 15, 8:30. Strollers welcome; nursing and diaper changing available.
