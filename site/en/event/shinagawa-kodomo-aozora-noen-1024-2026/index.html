Kids' Rooftop Farm: Vegetable Harvest (Shinagawa City Office)

Japanese title: 子ども青空農園（野菜収穫体験）（品川区役所第二庁舎屋上）

Shinagawa City Office Annex, Rooftop (Oimachi Sta.)

A vegetable-harvesting experience for children in the rooftop garden of the Shinagawa City Office. Two sessions (10:00-11:00, 11:30-12:30), 20 children each, first come; apply by phone or email. 800 yen. Cancelled in rain.
