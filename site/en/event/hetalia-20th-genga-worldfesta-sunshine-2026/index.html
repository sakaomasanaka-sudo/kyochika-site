Hetalia 20th Anniversary Original Art Exhibition WorldFesta

Japanese title: ヘタリア20周年原画展 WorldFesta

Sunshine City Exhibition Hall A (World Import Mart Bldg 4F) (Higashi-ikebukuro Sta.)

Original art exhibition for Hetalia's 20th anniversary, with pencil sketches, color illustrations, a life-size Italy statue, photo spots and limited goods. Timed-entry tickets; closes at 17:00 on the last day.
