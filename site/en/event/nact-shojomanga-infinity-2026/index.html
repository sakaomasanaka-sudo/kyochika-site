Shojo Manga Infinity: Moto Hagio x Ryoko Yamagishi x Waki Yamato

Japanese title: 少女漫画・インフィニティ 萩尾望都×山岸凉子×大和和紀 三人展

The National Art Center, Tokyo (Special Exhibition Gallery 2E) (Nogizaka Sta.)

An exhibition of three shojo manga legends with over 800 works planned, including original monochrome and color drawings. Closed Tuesdays; open until 20:00 on Fridays and Saturdays.
