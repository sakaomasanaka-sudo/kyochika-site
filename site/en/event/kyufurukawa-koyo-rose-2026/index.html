Kyu-Furukawa Gardens "Autumn Leaves and Roses, Japanese and Western Autumn"

Japanese title: 旧古河庭園「紅葉とバラ、和と洋の秋」

Kyu-Furukawa Gardens (Kami-Nakazato Sta.)

An autumn event at a garden where you can see autumn leaves and autumn roses together. Koto and pan flute concerts on Nov 28 at 12:00 and 15:00 (canceled in rain), and a rose fragrance workshop on Nov 24 (advance registration, 40 people). Also a temporary Rose Garden Market shop and a digital stamp rally. Last entry 16:30.
