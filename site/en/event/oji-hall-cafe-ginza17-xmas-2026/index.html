Ginza Burat Special #225 Café Ginza 17 (Christmas songs)

Japanese title: 銀座ぶらっとスペシャル#225 Caféギンザ17（クリスマス・ソング）

Composer-pianist Masanori Kato with the Harmonia Ensemble choir and others. Two shows: 13:30 matinee (women's choir, incl. a Christmas medley) and 18:30 soirée (men's and mixed choir, mostly Christmas songs such as 'Carol of the Bells' and 'Jingle Bell Rock').
