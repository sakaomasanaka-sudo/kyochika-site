12th Metropolitan Zoos Africa Fair (Tama Zoo)

Japanese title: 第12回都立動物園アフリカフェア（多摩動物公園）

African country booths, a BALANGOMA concert, an interactive dance stage, an African stamp rally and face painting. Opening ceremony with Osman Sankon on the 22nd.
