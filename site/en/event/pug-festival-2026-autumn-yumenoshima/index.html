Pug Fes 2026 AUTUMN at Eeno no Mori Dog Garden

Japanese title: パグフェス 2026 AUTUMN meets イーノの森Dog Garden

Eeno no Mori Dog Garden (Yumenoshima, Koto) (Shin-Kiba Sta.)

An autumn dog festival for owners of pugs and pug mixes, with a pug goods market. Held rain or shine (cancelled in storms). Each dog needs a ticket too.
