Turner from Tate: The Sublime in Painting and a Dialogue with Contemporary Art

Japanese title: テート美術館 ターナー展――崇高の絵画、現代美術との対話

The National Museum of Western Art (Ueno Sta.)

An exhibition centered on Turner works from Tate in the UK, tracing a dialogue between sublime painting and contemporary art. Open until 20:00 on Fridays and Saturdays (last entry 30 minutes before closing).
