Nihombashi Ebisu-ko Bettara-ichi Fair

Japanese title: 日本橋恵比寿講べったら市

Around Takarada Ebisu Shrine (Nihombashi-honcho, Odenmacho, Horidomecho) (Kodemmacho Sta.) · Free

An Edo-era street fair around Takarada Ebisu Shrine, where stalls sell bettara-zuke, a sweet pickled daikon radish, alongside food stalls. Portable shrine parades on the 19th and Bon dancing on the 20th. Held in rain except in storms.
