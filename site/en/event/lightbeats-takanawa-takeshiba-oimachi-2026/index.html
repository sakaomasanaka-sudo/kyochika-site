LIGHTBEATS (Takeshiba, Takanawa & Oimachi light installation)

Japanese title: LIGHTBEATS（竹芝・高輪・大井町 光と音のライトインスタレーション）

TAKANAWA GATEWAY CITY, WATERS takeshiba & OIMACHI TRACKS (Takanawa Gateway Sta.) · Free

A light-and-sound installation by JR East across three districts: Takeshiba, Takanawa and Oimachi. Walk through to create light and sound, wave your arms at a fountain to move the water and lights, and see floor projection mapping linked between the three sites. Hours are tentative.
