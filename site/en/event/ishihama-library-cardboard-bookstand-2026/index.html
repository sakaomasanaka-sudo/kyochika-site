Kids' Workshop: Cardboard Book Stand (Ishihama Library)

Japanese title: ワークショップ石浜工房 こども向け「ダンボールブックスタンド」（石浜図書館）

Ishihama Library 4F (Minami-Senju Sta.)

Draw on cardboard and decorate it with origami and stickers to make a book stand. Two sessions (10:00, 11:00), 4 kids each. Apply Oct 20–Nov 22 online or at the library counter (Taito library card needed; lottery).
