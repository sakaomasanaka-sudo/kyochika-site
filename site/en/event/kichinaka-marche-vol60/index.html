Kichi*Naka MARCHE Vol.60

Japanese title: Kichi＊Naka MARCHE Vol.60

Rebest Forum (Nakamichi-dori, Kichijoji) (Kichijoji Sta.)

The 60th edition of a market on Kichijoji's Nakamichi-dori, running since 2019: candles, fabric goods, embroidery, flower arrangements, rice-flour sweets, homemade jam and more. Makers differ by day.
