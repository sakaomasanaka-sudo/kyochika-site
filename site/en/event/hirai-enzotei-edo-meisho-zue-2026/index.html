Walking the Edo Meisho Zue (Hirai Enzotei)

Japanese title: 江戸名所図会を歩く（ひらい圓藏亭）

Hirai Enzotei (Hirai Sta.) · Free

A talk by Sadako Nakamura, who spent ten years walking the sites of the Edo-era guidebook Edo Meisho Zue. This time: Kan'eiji Temple and 200 years of change.
