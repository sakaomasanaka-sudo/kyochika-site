YORUNOYO 2026 (THE YOKOHAMA ILLUMINATION)

Japanese title: ヨルノヨ2026（THE YOKOHAMA ILLUMINATION）

Osanbashi Yokohama International Passenger Terminal, Yamashita Park & more (Nihon-odori Sta.) · Free

A large light-and-sound illumination across Yokohama's waterfront, including Osanbashi Pier and Yamashita Park, co-hosted by the City of Yokohama. Held every day during the period; free entry.
