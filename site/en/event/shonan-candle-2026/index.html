Shonan Candle 2026 (Enoshima Samuel Cocking Garden)

Japanese title: 湘南キャンドル2026（江の島サムエル・コッキング苑）

Enoshima Samuel Cocking Garden (Katase-Enoshima Sta.)

About 10,000 candles light up Enoshima for 25 days, with a collaboration with the anime "Rascal Does Not Dream" series and paid workshops such as painting candle cups. Garden entry is free until 17:00; the Sea Candle tower costs extra.
