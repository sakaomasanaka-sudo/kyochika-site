Kids Flea Market at COREDO Muromachi

Japanese title: キッズフリマ in コレド室町

Fukutoku no Mori (COREDO Muromachi) (Mitsukoshimae Sta.) · Free

A flea market where only children buy and sell with real money; adults cannot enter the selling area. Two sessions each day, 11:00-12:00 and 14:30-15:30. Shoppers can book priority entry (from 0:00 on October 20, first come) or just come on the day. Cancelled in rain; check the official site at 7:30 on the day.
