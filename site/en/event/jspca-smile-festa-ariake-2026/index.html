Zutto Issho! Smile Festa (rescue dog & cat adoption and more)

Japanese title: ずっといっしょ！スマイルフェスタ（保護いぬ・保護ねこ譲渡会ほか）

TFT Hall 500 (Ariake) (Kokusai-Tenjijo Sta.) · Free

By the Japan Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals: dog and cat adoption sessions (11:00-15:30 each day), talk shows, a family musical and disaster-prep workshops. No pets allowed in the venue.
