Furusato Christmas Market (Lighting Collaboration Kanamachi)

Japanese title: ふるさと・クリスマスマーケット（ライティング・コラボレーションかなまち）

Katsushika Niijuku Mirai Park (Kanamachi Sta.)

A one-day Christmas market with food and goods from popular shops and regional producers, a Christmas song stage and workshops (booking required). Held at Katsushika Niijuku Mirai Park for the first time, with science classes by Tokyo University of Science students (apply in advance).
