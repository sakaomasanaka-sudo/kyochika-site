Diwali Festa Nishi-Kasai

Judul bahasa Jepang: ディワリフェスタ西葛西

Takino Park (Stasiun Nishi-Kasai) · Gratis

Celebrate the Indian festival of lights in Nishi-Kasai, home to many Indian residents: classical dance and music on stage, curry and sweets, and an Indian bazaar. Now held at Takino Park.
