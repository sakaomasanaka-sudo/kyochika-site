Fun Train Day at Niijuku Traffic Park

Judul bahasa Jepang: わくわくドキドキ お楽しみトレインデー

Niijuku Traffic Park (Stasiun Kanamachi)

A special train runs around the traffic park and you can ride it. Break from noon to 13:00. Cancelled if it rains.

Tanggal
25 Okt (Min)
Jam
10:00–16:00
Harga
Free for preschoolers (with an adult); elementary ¥30 per ride, junior high and up ¥100
Tempat
